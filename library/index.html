The collection contains 967 Goodreads records from the September 6, 2026 snapshot. 296 have recovered cover images; other books use a title cover. Spine lettering is reconstructed for this room. The catalog is not connected to live Goodreads updates.

This is a collection of book records, ratings, and reading memories. Full book texts are not included.

Room modeled in Blender. Follow the driveway into the tiled foyer, then explore the library and the room behind the upstairs bookcase. Snow, old leather, a chess table, Shackleton’s Endurance, and a few Michigan State memories.

Subject sections are editorial. The catalog retains the original Goodreads reading status and ratings.
